## Platform

## Stack

## Users

A mixed audience of people buying flowers and plants: someone sending a gift for a birthday, anniversary, apology or any ordinary day; someone furnishing their own home with plants and pots; and businesses buying the same products as gifts or decor. Most arrive on a phone, often with a date or occasion in mind, and want to know quickly what they can order and how to reach the shop.

## Product Purpose

Harrison Flores is a florist ("floricultura"). The site presents the shop, its two service lines and how to order, and turns a visit into a contact (order request or question). Success: a visitor understands what Harrison Flores makes and reaches out to order.

## Product Principles

1. Ordering is the job: every page leads to one clear way to get in touch and order.
2. The flowers are the product; the interface stays out of their way.
3. Speak to every buyer (gift-giver, home, business) without splitting into separate funnels.
4. Honest by default: no claims the shop has not confirmed.
