## Scope
Whole marketing site: index.html (Início), servicos.html, sobre.html, contato.html. Mode: Persuade.

## Audience and action
Mixed buyers (gift-givers, home, businesses), mostly on phones. One action everywhere: "Pedir pelo WhatsApp" (wa.me link with pre-filled message). Proof: none real yet; labeled example testimonials only.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The premium florist canon, played straight: the flowers lead, the page is quiet white paper around them. Refuses busy shop-grid layouts, discount banners and stock card rows.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, dusty rose (#F3E3E0) fields owning whole sections, graphite ink (#2B2B2B), deep sage for small text and lines. Bodoni Moda display with optical sizing, Hanken Grotesk light for text. Fine-line botanical illustrations (graphite stroke, rose/sage watercolour washes offset under the lines) as the imagery system. Pill buttons with nested arrow circle.
STORY: Visitor sees a bouquet and one clear promise, understands they can get the right bouquet or plant for their occasion, sees how it works and what others say (examples), clears doubts in the FAQ, taps WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left 5/12 column with headline (≈5.5rem Bodoni), one-line subcopy, WhatsApp pill; right 7/12 a large line-art bouquet on a rose wash, bleeding toward the right edge. Floating pill nav on top. Mobile: headline, subcopy, CTA, then bouquet.
FORM: Canon (user-chosen standing exit), seed key bd900902 (degraded roll).
SIGNATURE: Benefits drawn as annotations on a botanical plate: one bouquet illustration with three leader-lined callouts. Motion: illustration strokes draw in once on first view; sections fade up from visible defaults.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
